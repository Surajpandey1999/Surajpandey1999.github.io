# Campaign Tracking

## UTM convention
Use:
`?utm_source=PLATFORM&utm_medium=SOCIAL&utm_campaign=CAMPAIGN&utm_content=FORMAT_COUNTRY`

Examples:
- `utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=global30&utm_content=research_india`
- `utm_source=reddit&utm_medium=community&utm_campaign=global30&utm_content=himalaya_nepal`
- `utm_source=x&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=global30&utm_content=aws_philippines`

## Weekly metrics
Track in Google Analytics:
- Users
- Sessions
- Engaged sessions
- Top countries
- Top landing pages
- Referral sources
- Article engagement
- Resume clicks
- Contact conversions
- GitHub outbound clicks

## Decision rules
- High impressions + low clicks: improve title/hook.
- High clicks + low engagement: improve article opening and internal links.
- High engagement + low conversion: strengthen CTA.
- One country/topic repeatedly outperforms: create a focused content series.

Never use tracking parameters to disguise unsolicited or automated spam.
