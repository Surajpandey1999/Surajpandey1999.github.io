# 30-Day Global Promotion Campaign

## Core content pillars
1. **Himalayan risk & public policy** — disaster preparedness, climate risk, early warning, India/Nepal.
2. **Technology & AI** — GenAI, LLMs, AWS, cloud, cybersecurity.
3. **Youth, education & careers** — exams, employability, skills, AI productivity.

## Country/community focus
### India
Students, AWS/cloud learners, public-policy communities, freshers, UPSC/competitive-exam discussions, technology communities.

### Nepal
Himalayan disaster risk, climate adaptation, hydrology, technology and student communities. Prioritize useful discussion after disasters; never exploit victims for promotion.

### Bangladesh
AI/cloud learners, university students, software and career communities.

### Philippines
IT careers, cloud/AWS, AI, developer and remote-work communities.

### Indonesia & Malaysia
AI, cloud, developer, university and professional communities. Adapt wording to local context and use English where accepted.

### Singapore, Vietnam, Sri Lanka, Pakistan, Thailand
Professional technology, student, research and policy communities.

### Japan & South Korea
Use concise, technically useful English unless a trusted local-language contributor can localize the post. Do not machine-spam translations.

## Platform playbook
### LinkedIn
Teach, analyze and show project evidence. Use 1 strong idea, 3–5 short paragraphs, and one relevant article/project link.

### Reddit
Participate before promoting. Follow subreddit rules. Prefer answers and discussion posts; link only when the article materially adds evidence.

### X
Use concise threads, charts/data points and strong questions. Avoid repeated links.

### GitHub
Turn technical learning into reproducible projects, READMEs and documentation. Cross-link portfolio pages naturally.

### Facebook/Discord/Telegram
Only post in communities that permit self-promotion. Prefer weekly value posts and community participation over repetitive advertising.

### Quora
Answer specific questions with original explanation and cite your article only when it provides additional detail.

## AI repurposing prompt
Given a portfolio article, create:
1. LinkedIn insight post
2. X thread of 5–8 posts
3. Reddit discussion draft
4. Instagram carousel outline of 7 slides
5. Quora answer
6. GitHub project/documentation idea
7. SEO title and meta description

Requirements: factual, non-clickbait, original wording for every platform, no invented statistics, no unsolicited DM, no spam, and preserve source citations.
